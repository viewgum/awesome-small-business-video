# Awesome Small Business Video [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

Official, free resources for a small business making short vertical videos for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and
Facebook Reels: each app's own help, music a business may use, the rules for ads, endorsements, reviews and AI labels, and free
editing apps and footage. Every entry is the publisher's own page (a US regulation is linked at Cornell's Legal Information
Institute, which publishes its text), described only with what that page says; the day each was read is in
[sources.json](sources.json). No affiliate links.

## Each app's help for businesses

- [TikTok for Business](https://www.tiktok.com/business/en-US) - TikTok's site for businesses, with Ads Manager, the business account, Business Center and creative tools.
- [TikTok for Business Blog](https://www.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog) - TikTok's announcements, guides and marketing research for businesses.
- [Get started creating YouTube Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070) - YouTube's help for making Shorts up to 3 minutes long, and for uploading vertical videos from a phone or computer as Shorts.
- [Making it easier to create videos on Facebook](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/06/making-it-easier-create-videos-facebook/) - Meta's June 2025 note that all videos on Facebook will be shared as reels, with no length or format restrictions.
- [Manage your Business Profile photos and videos](https://support.google.com/business/answer/6103862) - Google's help for adding photos and videos of a storefront, products and services to a Business Profile, which show on Google once the profile is verified.

## Music a business may use

A song in an app's general library is not always cleared for a business account or an ad. These are the libraries that say what
a business may do.

- [TikTok Commercial Music Library](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/music/pc/en) - TikTok's pre-cleared music for organic content and ad creation, searchable by theme, genre, mood and length.
- [YouTube Audio Library](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3376882) - YouTube's help for the music and sound effects in YouTube Studio's Audio Library, the only ones it says it knows to be copyright-safe; Creative Commons tracks must be credited in the video's description.
- [Incompetech music licenses](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/licenses/) - Music free under Creative Commons with credit, or under a Standard License for uses where credit is not wanted or impossible, such as radio and TV ads.
- [Free Music Archive FAQ](https://freemusicarchive.org/faq) - How to find each track's Creative Commons license and what NonCommercial means before using a track in a business video.

## Ads, endorsements, reviews and AI labels

- [FTC's Endorsement Guides: What People Are Asking](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking) - The US Federal Trade Commission's answers on when a paid or gifted endorsement has to be disclosed, and how.
- [16 CFR 465.2, fake or false consumer reviews](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/465.2) - The US rule that bars a business from writing, buying or spreading fake reviews and testimonials.
- [TikTok Branded Content Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/bc-policy/en) - TikTok's rules for branded content, which must be posted with the commercial content disclosure toggle on.
- [YouTube branded content disclosure](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/154235) - YouTube's help for the paid promotion setting, which adds a disclosure label at the start of the video.
- [Disclosing use of GenAI content on YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491) - YouTube's rule that creators disclose realistic AI-generated or altered content, such as a real person shown saying something they did not say.
- [ASA and CAP influencers' guide](https://www.asa.org.uk/resource/influencers-guide.html) - The UK advertising regulator's guide to labelling ads clearly on social media, with affiliate marketing disclosure.

## Free editing apps

- [DaVinci Resolve](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve) - Blackmagic Design's editor, with a free version and a paid Studio version.
- [Edits by Instagram](https://edits.com/) - Instagram's video creation app, with captions, a teleprompter, templates and audio tools.
- [CapCut Terms of Service](https://www.capcut.com/clause/terms-of-service) - CapCut's terms, which say commercial use of its templates, clips, fonts and effects depends on the product and its Materials License Agreement, and that no rights to its music are licensed except as stated.
- [Kdenlive](https://kdenlive.org/) - Free and open source video editor.
- [Shotcut](https://shotcut.org/) - Free, open source video editor for Windows, Mac and Linux.
